Cheesecake

Description

Filling cheesecake with healthy quark and lotus flavor

Ingredients:

  • 1L of milk
  • 250g of quark
  • 200g of raspberries

  • Cake base:

    • 100g of butter
    • 500g of flour
    • 100ml of milk

Steps:

  1. Stirr everything up
  2. Bake on 250 °C for 10 minutes
  3. after 10 minutes lower the temperature to 200 °C and bake another 10 minutes
